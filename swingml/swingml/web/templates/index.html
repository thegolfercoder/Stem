Getting a clip it can read
  • Face on works best. Stand the phone square to where the ball is going, level with the golfer. Down the line, from behind, is harder: the body hides itself and the analysis will often say so rather than guess.
  • Whole body in shot, all the way through. Including the club going up. Standing further back beats filling the frame and losing the feet.
  • Record a bit either side. Start before the takeaway, stop after the finish is held. A second each way is plenty — a long clip is fine, it just takes longer.
  • Any frame rate. 30, 60, 240, slow motion. It reads the real frame times rather than trusting the label, so it does not need telling.
  • A tilted phone is fine. Camera roll is measured and removed.

A clip it cannot read gets refused with a reason, not filled in with a guess.
