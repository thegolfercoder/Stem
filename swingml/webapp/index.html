Golfer is

The first clip you analyse also downloads the body-tracking model, about 30 MB. After that it is cached and every later clip starts straight away.

Getting a clip it can read
  • Face on works best. Phone square to where the ball is going, level with the golfer. Down the line, from behind, is harder: the body hides itself, and it will often say so rather than guess.
  • Whole body in shot, the whole way through. Standing further back beats filling the frame and losing the feet at the top.
  • Record a bit either side — before the takeaway, until the finish is held.
  • iPhone users: if nothing decodes, your clip is HEVC. Safari reads it; otherwise set Camera → Formats → Most Compatible.

What to try:

Being told this is better than being handed numbers from a clip that did not contain a swing. A wrong number gets acted on; a missing one gets looked into.

Tempo

How long each half took, straight from the frame times. These are the strongest numbers here, which is why they come first.

The swing, start to finish

The whole motion to scale, then what happened in each part. Every figure is one of the measurements on this page; nothing is added that was not measured.

The eight positions

Where the model put each one, and how far out it has been measured to be. Click any frame to see it full size, then step through them with the arrow keys.

* Toe-up and mid-follow-through are defined by the club shaft being horizontal. A body-pose estimator does not see the club, so these two are placed from the body's motion rather than observed directly.

Rotation at the top

How far the shoulders and hips had turned away from square to the camera, measured from how much they foreshorten. It carries no sign — turning towards the camera and away from it look identical — and it reads low against reality, so compare it with your own swings from the same spot rather than a published figure.

Stability

Movement measured against the ground, in units of your own body length, so no calibration is needed.
