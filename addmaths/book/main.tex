\chapter*{Preface}
This book follows the Cambridge IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606) syllabus through all fourteen units.
Part~I teaches each topic: the theory, the formulas, the errors that cost marks and a one-page summary.
Part~II works the written examples and exam-style questions in full, with mark schemes.
Part~III gives a worked instance of every type of question, once for each method it can call for.
Part~IV is a bank of graded exercises, and Part~V solves every one of them step by step.
The exercises are produced by the same generators as the practice engine on the companion website,
and every generated answer is checked independently before the book is built.
Cambridge past papers are copyright and are not reproduced here.
