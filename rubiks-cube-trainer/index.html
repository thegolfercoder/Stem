Rubik's cube trainer

Drag the background to turn the cube round. Drag a face to turn that layer. Or use the keyboard: U D L R F B, with Shift for anticlockwise.

Solve it

The first uses Kociemba's two-phase algorithm and finds about twenty moves. The second uses the layer-by-layer method and finds about a hundred and fifty, every one of which belongs to a step you could follow yourself.
